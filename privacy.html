Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Smartech Garage Door Repair ("Smartech", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or request service, including through our Facebook and Instagram ads and forms, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address and ZIP code or service address.

How we use your information

  • To contact you by phone, text message or email about your service request, quote or appointment.

How we share your information

We share your information only with the local technicians and service partners who perform the service you requested, and with service providers that help us run our business (for example, call tracking and scheduling tools). We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages

Data retention and your choices
