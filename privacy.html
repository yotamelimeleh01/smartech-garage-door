Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Smartech Garage Door Repair ("Smartech", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or request service, including through our website and our Facebook and Instagram ads and forms, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address and ZIP code or service address.
  • Details about your service request, such as the type of garage door problem and your preferred time.
  • Call and text records when you call or text our phone numbers. Calls may be recorded for quality assurance.
  • Basic website usage data, such as pages visited, collected through cookies and similar tools.

How we use your information

  • To contact you by phone, text message or email about your service request, quote or appointment.
  • To schedule and perform garage door repair, maintenance and installation services.
  • To improve our service and measure how our advertising performs.

How we share your information

We share your information only with the technicians and service partners who perform the service you requested, and with service providers that help us run our business (for example, call tracking, scheduling and email tools). We do not sell your personal information.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, you agree that we may call or text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time.

Data retention and your choices

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide service and meet legal obligations. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, by calling or texting us.
